How Cohen's Kappa Is Calculated

Raw agreement is the share of matched items where both annotators chose the same rating.

Expected agreement is the agreement we would expect by chance from each annotator's rating distribution.

Cohen's kappa is calculated as: kappa = (observed agreement - expected agreement) / (1 - expected agreement).

A kappa of 1 means perfect agreement. A kappa near 0 means agreement is close to chance. Negative values mean agreement is worse than chance.
